---

name: deep-research
description: Research a question in depth across the web and the user's own documents and messages, then answer with cited sources. Use for "research", "dig into", "everything about", "what do I know about".
---

1. Split the question into two to four smaller questions you can look up.
2. For each one, search the user's own data first with search_my_stuff, then the web (web search, then
   fetch_page on the two or three most useful pages). Use who_is before looking for one person's messages.
3. Cross-reference: when two sources disagree, say so and say which is newer.
4. Answer out loud in at most five sentences, the finding first. Then say how many sources you used.
   The sources go on the Result card by themselves; do not read links aloud.
5. Cite in the text you write as [1], [2] in the order you used them. Never cite something you did not
   read. If nothing was found, say so plainly and suggest where else to look.
