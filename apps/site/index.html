01 Hear

It listens on your Mac, not on a server.

On this Mac · 350 ms to the final transcript

Wake phrase or hotkey
Say “Hey Markon”, or just “Hey Mark”, or hold a key. A short list of spoken commands works with no model at all: hide, tidy up, clear the screen, settings.
Patient with pauses
It waits longer when your sentence stops mid-clause, so thinking out loud does not get cut off.
Audio is thrown away
Speech becomes text on the device and the sound is discarded. Only the words go to the model.

02 Speak

Answers start before the thought is finished.

Each sentence is spoken the moment it is complete, by a voice that runs on your Mac. The voice is verified on every start. If a file changes, Markon refuses to speak rather than sound like someone else. There is no fallback voice.

179 ms to the first word · spoken on your Mac

04 Act

It does the thing. You say yes first.

Markon can run your Shortcuts, set volume and Wi-Fi, lay out windows, drive Chrome in its own profile, and work any app through Accessibility. Anything that changes something stops on a card with the exact text, and waits for your word.

09 Private

You can always see what it did.

  • Your voice, discarded after transcription
  • Keys, in the macOS keychain only
  • Memory: only what you said to remember
  • Passwords and card numbers, never typed
  • The words of a turn, to the model you picked
  • Tool results, scrubbed of keys and tokens
  • Files, only when a tool reads one, named on its card
  • Crash reports, only if you opt in
